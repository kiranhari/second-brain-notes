# Constraints drive creativity more than freedom

When given too many choices, people often produce worse creative work than when given specific constraints. This is because constraints force lateral thinking and eliminate the "paradox of choice" — the cognitive paralysis that comes from having too many options.

This pattern appears across domains:
- Writers who produce better work under tight word limits
- Designers who solve harder problems with strict brand guidelines
- Musicians who write their best songs when limited to a single instrument
- Chefs who create brilliant dishes when given only five ingredients

This applies to: writing, design, product development, parenting, engineering.
