# Example — Capture Note (Highlight)
*This is a demo file illustrating what a well-formed "knowledge building block" looks like per [[Chapter 4 - Capture - Keep What Resonates]] — not a real captured note. Delete or repurpose freely.*

> [!quote] Highlight
> "Information becomes knowledge—personal, embodied, verified—only when we put it to use."

**Source:** *Building a Second Brain*, Tiago Forte, Chapter 3 ("How a Second Brain Works")
**Why I saved this:** Resonated immediately — it's the clearest one-line answer yet to "why bother organizing notes at all." Ties directly to the retrieval-vs-capture tension flagged across Chapters 1-3.
**Capture criteria it meets:** Surprising (reframes "knowledge" as something earned through use, not accumulation) + Useful (a reusable filter for judging any PKM technique going forward).

---

### What makes this a good capture note (per the book's own guidance)
- **Source info included** (title, author, chapter) — so you can relocate it later without re-reading.
- **Only the highlight, not the whole chapter** — follows the "no more than ~10% of the source" guideline.
- **A one-line "why I saved this"** — this is the distillation step starting early (previewed in Ch.3, formalized in Ch.6), answering "how is this useful to my future self?"
- **Tagged against a capture criterion** — makes it easy to later ask "did my captures actually follow my own stated filters?"
