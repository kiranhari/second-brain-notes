# 00-Inbox — Rules

**Rule:** everything captured goes here first. Don't classify, don't rename perfectly, don't tag. Just get it in. Sort during your weekly review.

See [[Chapter 2 - PARA Folder System#The Inbox Rule Capture Now Organize Later|Chapter 2 notes]] for the full reasoning.

## Example of a raw capture (from the book)
```
# [Inbox] Note on Deep Work — Podcast Clip
From: Knowledge Project podcast, Episode 147
Guest: Cal Newport
Key idea: "Attention residue" — every time you switch tasks, part of your
attention stays on the previous task for minutes afterward. This compounds
across a day of context-switching into a major cognitive tax.
Follow up: [[Deep Work Book Notes]]? Or add to [[03-Resources/Focus and Productivity]]?
```
Note it doesn't need to be resolved immediately — the bracketed questions are just placeholders for your weekly review.
