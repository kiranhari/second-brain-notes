# Knowledge Management — Area Overview

Ongoing responsibility, no end date: keeping this vault alive and useful (weekly review, pruning stale notes, evolving the PARA structure as life changes). This is distinct from [[01-Projects/Second Brain Build-Out/Project Overview|Second Brain Build-Out]], which is the one-time project of creating the initial content — once that project finishes, *maintaining* the system becomes this Area's job.
